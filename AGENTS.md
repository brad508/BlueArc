# BlueArc Content OS

BlueArc is a premium sleep-health brand. This system supports high-volume social video production.

- Human taste and clarity matter more than flashy AI effects.
- Avoid generic AI animations, gratuitous motion, excessive emojis/icons, and overediting.
- Keep the person speaking as the primary visual unless graphics meaningfully clarify the idea.
- Edits should feel modern, native, premium, and restrained.
- Do not make unsupported medical claims. Flag claims for human review.
- Preserve reusable lessons from approved edits in skills/ and docs/; do not treat unapproved tests as brand guidance.
- Use the existing isolated checkout; do not create worktrees unless requested.
- The pipeline test is only an environment test, not creative direction.
- Do not proceed to transcription, branding, production templates, or automation without user instructions.
- Keep raw footage and exports out of Git. Never commit credentials.
